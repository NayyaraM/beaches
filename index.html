Cedar Grove Beach, Long Island, NY

Cedar Grove Beach in Staten Island is one of NYC’s most peaceful shoreline spots, offering a quiet stretch of sand tucked between Great Kills Park and New Dorp Beach. It’s known for its calm, neighborhood feel—far less crowded than South Beach or Midland Beach—and features soft sand, gentle waves, seasonal lifeguards, and simple amenities like restrooms. Visitors often describe it as clean, relaxed, and ideal for families or anyone looking to escape the busier city beaches. With views across Lower New York Bay and a laid‑back atmosphere, it’s a great place for a low‑key swim, a walk along the water, or a quiet afternoon.
