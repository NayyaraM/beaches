Beach Water Quality and Pollution in NYC


Project Overview:

We want to spread awareness about the water quality in beaches in New York, sharing and encouraging New Yorkers to take care of the beaches and reduce sea pollution. Our targeted demographic is beach goers, who deserve to know the quality of the waters they swim in, environmentalists to raise awareness and support their cause and New Yorkers as this is their waters.
