Cedar Grove Beach, Long Island, NY

Cedar Grove Beach in Staten Island is one of NYC’s most peaceful shoreline spots, offering a quiet stretch of sand tucked between Great Kills Park and New Dorp Beach. It’s known for its calm, neighborhood feel—far less crowded than South Beach or Midland Beach—and features soft sand, gentle waves, seasonal lifeguards, and simple amenities like restrooms. Visitors often describe it as clean, relaxed, and ideal for families or anyone looking to escape the busier city beaches. With views across Lower New York Bay and a laid‑back atmosphere, it’s a great place for a low‑key swim, a walk along the water, or a quiet afternoon.

Wolfe's Pond Beach, Staten Island, NY

Wolfe’s Pond Beach is a quiet, tucked‑away shoreline on Staten Island’s southern edge, offering a very different vibe from the larger, busier NYC beaches. It’s often described as one of the city’s least‑crowded beaches, sitting along Raritan Bay and Prince’s Bay inside Wolfe’s Pond Park. The beach faces sheltered bay waters rather than the open Atlantic, which gives it a calmer, more intimate feel. Visitors highlight its natural setting, with seaweed, marine life, and a more rugged shoreline compared to places like South Beach or Midland Beach. The surrounding park adds miles of walking paths, forested areas, ponds, and recreation spaces, making it a great spot for a mix of beach time and nature exploration.
